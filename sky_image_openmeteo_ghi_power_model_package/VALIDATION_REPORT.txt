ASSUMPTIONS / LIMITATIONS
- Tilt 25 degrees; azimuth 180 degrees (south); albedo 0.2.
- Capacity unset. Without prior calibration/history, 1000 kW is explicitly marked as a
  placeholder. Prior-power capacity estimates are also labelled as estimates.
- Temperature coefficient -0.004/C; default multiplicative loss factor 0.82.
- Site timezone Asia/Kolkata; image-core UTC offset 5.5 hours.
- Confirm WhatsApp recording time; filename time can be share time. Video elapsed time
  assumes ordinary constant-frame-rate footage, not time-lapse.
- Nearest actual matching tolerance 8 minutes. Dates day-first unless ISO/explicit.
- Unlabelled power magnitude guesses can be wrong; set power_unit or label units.
  V/I input values are assumed volts and amperes. GHI inputs must be global horizontal.
- Sky/future CSI, temperature and confidence are heuristic, not trained probabilistic
  forecasts. Image rules are retained. Cloud/humidity features are exported but no
  additional learned fusion method was added.
- Open-Meteo hourly radiation is a preceding-hour average, interpolated approximately.
  Retrieved/reanalysis historical weather is not a strict as-issued forecast backtest.
- Inputs/models is scanned but reserved; arbitrary serialized models are not loaded.
- Error/bias = actual minus prediction. MAE/RMSE use matched pairs. Accuracy is clipped
  to 0–100 and omitted at actual GHI <=50 W/m2 or power <=1 kW. Sample counts are given.
